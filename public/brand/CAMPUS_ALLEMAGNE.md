- `campus-allemagne-logo-approved.png`: exact approved horizontal logo, cropped from the user-approved master artwork. This is the only full logo used by the website.
- `campus-allemagne-symbol-approved.png`: exact compact crop from the same approved master for small UI placements and installed-app metadata.
