# AlmaGo — Homepage V2 Professional Plan

**Status:** IMPLEMENTED + VERIFIED  
**Date:** 23 September 2026  
**Scope:** public homepage only (`/`)  
**Relation to Master Plan:** professional visual refinement only. No new business capability. A38/A43/A44/A45 remain unchanged.

---

## 1. Objective

The homepage is the most important public page of AlmaGo.

Within a few seconds, a new visitor must understand:

1. AlmaGo is for students preparing a study project in Germany.
2. AlmaGo helps organise the profile, documents, orientation, procedures and applications.
3. The service is serious, structured and transparent.
4. The next action is obvious.
5. AlmaGo does not pretend to be a university, visa authority or admission decision-maker.

### Primary conversion
`Créer mon dossier`

### Secondary conversion
`Voir comment ça marche`

### Optional contextual action
`Explorer les programmes` only when the public programme discovery route is genuinely ready and useful.

---

## 2. Reference fusion

AlmaGo must combine the strongest qualities of TLScontact and uni-assist without copying their proprietary visual identity, logos, illustrations or page composition.

### From TLScontact
Keep:
- institutional clarity;
- blue/white trust language;
- one dominant action;
- strong hero hierarchy;
- very clear page sections;
- calm spacing;
- obvious process guidance;
- restrained interface chrome.

Do not copy:
- TLScontact logo;
- exact hero composition;
- Brandenburg Gate artwork;
- exact buttons, flags or proprietary imagery.

### From uni-assist
Keep:
- human photography;
- visible international-student context;
- emotional warmth;
- a clear six-step journey;
- educational guidance;
- friendly visual storytelling;
- strong Germany-study context.

Do not copy:
- uni-assist logo;
- red circle identity;
- yellow brand system;
- proprietary photography;
- exact six-step carousel design.

### AlmaGo result
The page must feel:
- professional like TLScontact;
- human like uni-assist;
- more modern than both;
- calmer and cleaner;
- clearly AlmaGo;
- designed by a real product team, not like a generic template.

## 3. Visual identity

### Core palette

- Primary blue: `#29308B`
- Strong blue: `#20266F`
- Human warm accent: `#E9652C`
- Accent dark: `#A74413`
- Main warm background: `#F7F6F3`
- White surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Muted surface: `#F3F1EC`
- Main text: `#172033`
- Accessible muted text: slate tone meeting WCAG AA
- Borders: subtle cool-neutral

### Design principles
- white space before decoration;
- one strong image at a time;
- no visual clutter;
- no fake institutional seals;
- no fake partner logos;
- no fake statistics;
- no generic stock-image wall;
- use human photography only where it strengthens trust.

### Typography
- clean sans-serif;
- strong hierarchy;
- large hero title;
- short paragraphs;
- generous line-height;
- no decorative typeface;
- restrained uppercase labels only.

### Shape language
- medium radius controls;
- professional cards;
- subtle shadows;
- avoid excessive pills and glassmorphism.

### Motion
- subtle hover lift;
- 150–200 ms transitions;
- optional soft entrance motion;
- respect `prefers-reduced-motion`;
- no autoplay carousel.

## 4. Photography strategy

Photography is a major part of Homepage V2.

### What the images must show
Prefer:
- real international students;
- student/campus life;
- Germany-specific academic context;
- natural expressions;
- authentic environment;
- diversity without looking staged;
- architecture only when it supports the study story.

### Image rule
The homepage should use **2–4 strong images maximum**, not many small random photos.

### Planned visual use
1. Hero: human-first image with a student + German academic/city context.
2. Six-step section: optional supporting human image.
3. Product preview: real AlmaGo interface, not a stock image.
4. Trust/story section: optional second human image if composition benefits.

### Licensing
Use only:
- Unsplash;
- Pexels;
- Wikimedia Commons with compatible licensing;
- AlmaGo-owned/generated visual assets.

Every external image must have a known source/licence before merge.

### Section 3 — Why AlmaGo

Eyebrow:
`Pourquoi AlmaGo`

Heading:
`Un dossier plus clair, du premier document à la candidature.`

Four cards:

1. **Votre profil structuré**  
   Parcours académique et projet d’études regroupés au même endroit.

2. **Vos documents suivis**  
   Fichiers envoyés, statut et éventuelles demandes de correction visibles.

3. **Votre orientation organisée**  
   Programmes et recommandations présentés avec des critères lisibles.

4. **Vos candidatures lisibles**  
   Statut, échéance, prochaine action et historique accessibles au même endroit.

Design:
- 4 columns desktop;
- 2×2 tablet;
- one column mobile;
- simple consistent icons;
- light surface depth.

### Section 4 — Germany journey in six steps

Eyebrow:
`Votre parcours Allemagne`

`Votre projet d’études en 6 étapes claires.`

- optional supporting human image.

Mobile:

- no horizontal-scroll dependency.

Truth rule:
AlmaGo organises and tracks. It never claims to make admission or visa decisions.

## 6. Responsive requirements

### Desktop
- generous max width;
- strong visual composition;
- no empty giant gaps;
- image/text balance.

### Tablet
- preserve hierarchy;
- cards become 2 columns;
- hero can remain split if legible.

### Mobile
- one clear action at a time;
- no horizontal overflow;
- readable title;
- large tap targets;
- images cropped intentionally;
- six-step section becomes vertical;
- sticky header remains compact.

Target widths:
- 320 px;
- 375 px;
- 390 px;
- 768 px;
- 1024 px;
- 1440 px.

## 7. Accessibility requirements

Mandatory before merge:
- WCAG AA contrast;
- visible focus states;
- meaningful alt text;
- decorative images use empty alt when appropriate;
- correct heading hierarchy;
- no keyboard traps;
- 44 px minimum practical touch targets;
- `prefers-reduced-motion`;
- axe serious/critical violations = zero.

- hero image responsive sizes;
- avoid multiple priority images;
- lazy-load non-critical imagery;
- do not load video in hero;

- Lighthouse remains within existing AlmaGo advisory budgets.
