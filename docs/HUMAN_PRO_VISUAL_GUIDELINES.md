# AlmaGo — Human Pro visual & editorial guidelines

## Purpose

AlmaGo should feel institutional, calm and trustworthy **without feeling empty or impersonal**.

The visual system combines:

- procedural clarity;
- human presence;
- practical guidance;
- visible sources and limits;
- restrained editorial photography.

The objective is not to look like a startup landing page. It is to make a complex student journey feel understandable and accompanied.

## Photography

### Use

Prefer images that show:

- students in real study contexts;
- libraries, campuses, notebooks, laptops and preparation;
- natural expressions and natural light;
- moments of concentration, orientation or transition;
- diverse students without turning diversity into a decorative claim.

### Avoid

Do not use:

- handshake / corporate meeting clichés;
- call-centre imagery;
- exaggerated celebration or “visa approved” symbolism;
- staged passport, embassy or border-control scenes;
- generic startup teams looking at dashboards;
- photos that imply AlmaGo is a university, public authority or official visa intermediary.

### Density

- Public homepage: one strong hero image + one editorial support image is enough.
- Student space: imagery should be secondary to the dossier and next action.
- Admin space: normally no editorial photography.
- Long professional pages: use a photo only when it supports context or reassurance.

### Cropping

- Keep faces away from overlay text.
- Prefer portrait/vertical crops for hero surfaces.
- Prefer landscape crops for guidance panels.
- Keep the subject visible at mobile and desktop breakpoints.
- Use soft radius and restrained borders; no decorative floating-image stacks.

## Accessibility

Every meaningful image needs:

- useful alt text describing the scene, not marketing;
- enough contrast for any overlaid panel;
- no critical information conveyed by the image alone.

Decorative imagery should use an empty alt attribute.

## Licensing

- Use only imagery with a verified commercial-use license.
- Store the source and author when practical.
- Do not copy imagery from uni-assist, TLScontact or other benchmark sites.
- Benchmark their information hierarchy and human tone, not their assets.

## Editorial voice

Prefer:

- “Nous vous aidons à organiser…”
- “Voici ce qui compte maintenant.”
- “Vous avancez étape par étape.”
- “Cette étape sert à…”
- “Les informations restent reliées à leur source.”

Avoid:

- “Nous garantissons…”
- “Votre admission est assurée…”
- “Votre visa sera obtenu…”
- invented advisors, case workers or team members;
- implying an official partnership or institutional authority.

## Human guidance pattern

A good AlmaGo page should answer four questions:

1. Où suis-je ?
2. Pourquoi cette étape compte-t-elle ?
3. Que dois-je faire maintenant ?
4. Qui prend la décision finale ?

## Benchmark rule

Uni-assist may be used as a benchmark for balancing procedure, tools and human guidance. AlmaGo must keep its own visual identity, wording, information architecture and legal role.
