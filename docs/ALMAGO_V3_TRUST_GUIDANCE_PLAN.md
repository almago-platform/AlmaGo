# AlmaGo V3 — Trust, Guidance & Human Service Plan

**Status:** APPROVED PLAN — SAVED BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION  
**Date:** 24 September 2026  
**Scope:** Homepage + Student Space + Admin Space + public guidance layer  
**Relation to existing plans:** extension of Homepage V2, Student Space V2 and Admin Space V2. No replacement of the existing Master Plan. No new legal claim, partnership claim, admission guarantee or unsupported business capability.

---

## 1. Objective

AlmaGo V3 must move from:

> a professional student website

to:

> a trustworthy digital service that guides an international student through a study project in Germany.

The target experience combines:

- the institutional confidence and procedural seriousness associated with TLScontact;
- the educational clarity and guidance associated with uni-assist;
- AlmaGo's own advantage: a personal dossier, personalised next steps, clear deadlines and human follow-up.

The objective is not to imitate another website.

The objective is to make AlmaGo feel like a real, established student service that students can understand and trust immediately.

## 2. Central language rule

This rule is mandatory for all future AlmaGo public and student-facing copy.

### AlmaGo must sound like a real service, not like a technology product.

Avoid product/technology jargon when a normal student service would use a simpler expression.

### Avoid by default

- intelligence artificielle;
- AI / IA;
- chatbot;
- automation;
- workflow;
- dashboard;
- pipeline;
- engine;
- algorithm;
- scoring;
- smart system;
- knowledge base;
- dictionary;
- glossary as a cold technical label;
- case management;
- SaaS;
- backend;
- telemetry;
- analytics;
- generated recommendation;
- AI-powered;
- automated decision.

These words may still exist internally in technical documentation when technically necessary.

They should not be used in student-facing language unless the concept itself must legally or functionally be disclosed.

### Prefer human and institutional language

Instead of:
- `Dashboard`

Prefer:
- `Mon dossier`
- `Vue d'ensemble`
- `Où en est mon dossier ?`

Instead of:
- `Workflow`

Prefer:
- `Étapes de votre dossier`
- `Parcours`
- `Prochaines démarches`

Instead of:
- `AI recommendation`

Prefer:
- `Suggestion d'orientation`
- `Programmes qui correspondent à votre projet`
- `Pistes à examiner`

Instead of:
- `Glossaire / dictionnaire`

Prefer:
- `Comprendre les démarches`
- `Les mots à connaître`
- `Besoin d'une explication ?`

Instead of:
- `Knowledge base`

Prefer:
- `Centre d'aide`
- `Guides et démarches`

Instead of:
- `Case management`

Prefer:
- `Suivi de votre dossier`

### Voice

AlmaGo should sound:
- calm;
- clear;
- respectful;
- reassuring;
- concrete;
- serious;
- transparent;
- human.

Never childish.
Never over-friendly.
Never exaggerated.
Never salesy.
Never robotic.

## 3. Copy principle: convince through clarity, not slogans

Professional trust comes from concrete statements.

Avoid:
> La plateforme intelligente qui révolutionne vos études.

Prefer:
> Préparez votre projet d'études en Allemagne avec un dossier clair, des étapes compréhensibles et un suivi organisé.

Avoid:
> Notre technologie analyse votre profil.

Prefer:
> Les informations de votre dossier nous permettent de vous présenter des pistes adaptées à votre projet.

Avoid:
> Maximisez vos chances d'admission.

Prefer:
> Identifiez les programmes à examiner et préparez votre dossier dans les délais.

### Golden rule

Every important message should answer at least one real student question:

- Qu'est-ce que je dois faire ?
- Pourquoi ?
- Avant quand ?
- Où trouver l'information officielle ?
- Que fait AlmaGo maintenant ?
- Qu'est-ce qui se passe ensuite ?
- Est-ce que quelque chose manque ?
- Qui peut m'aider ?

## 5. V3 strategic pillars

### P1 — Institutional trust

Create visible evidence that AlmaGo is structured and serious.

Elements:
- clear identity and contact information;
- transparent role of AlmaGo;
- privacy and data handling explanations;
- official-source references;
- last verification dates where meaningful;
- clear difference between AlmaGo information and official university decisions;
- professional support process;
- consistent terminology.

### P2 — Personal guidance

The student should always understand their situation.

Elements:
- next action;
- next deadline;
- current AlmaGo follow-up;
- what is complete;
- what is waiting;
- what comes after;
- clear responsibility: student / AlmaGo / university.

### P3 — Verified information

Important programme and university information should show its origin whenever practical.

Recommended pattern:

**Source officielle**  
Nom de l'université ou organisme  
**Vérifié le :** date  
**Ce que cela signifie pour votre dossier :** short practical explanation  
CTA: `Voir la source officielle`

Never display a verification date unless the information was really reviewed.

### P4 — Human service

The site should show that students are being accompanied, not merely using software.

Possible elements when real:
- team page;
- named support contact;
- genuine staff photos;
- “Qui suit mon dossier ?”;
- response expectations;
- help panel;
- support history.

Never invent staff, photos, testimonials or support availability.

### P5 — Procedural clarity

Every major journey should be represented as clear steps.

Use language such as:
- `Votre parcours`
- `Étape actuelle`
- `Prochaine étape`
- `À faire maintenant`
- `AlmaGo vérifie actuellement`
- `En attente de l'université`

## 6. Homepage V3 additions

Homepage V2 remains the visual foundation.

V3 adds deeper trust and guidance.

### H1 — Stronger hero promise

The hero should communicate:
- study project in Germany;
- structured dossier;
- clear steps;
- personalised follow-up;
- one obvious CTA.

No technology terminology.

### H2 — “Comment AlmaGo vous accompagne”

A human 4–6 step explanation:

1. Créez votre dossier.
2. Ajoutez les informations utiles à votre projet.
3. Préparez vos documents.
4. Examinez les programmes qui correspondent à votre situation.
5. Suivez vos démarches et candidatures.
6. Gardez vos prochaines étapes et échéances au même endroit.

### H3 — Trust section

Possible title:
`Un accompagnement clair, avec des informations vérifiables`

Explain:
- official sources are linked when available;
- AlmaGo does not replace university decisions;
- the student keeps visibility over the dossier;
- sensitive documents are handled in the private space.

### H4 — “Notre rôle”

A short, strong section:

**AlmaGo vous aide à organiser votre projet.**  
We help structure information, documents, orientation and application follow-up.

**Les décisions restent officielles.**  
Admissions, university requirements and authority decisions remain with the competent institution.

This improves trust rather than weakening the offer.

### H5 — Human proof

Only real proof:
- real team;
- real service numbers;
- real user numbers;
- real partner logos;
- real testimonials.

If unavailable, use process evidence instead:
- documented steps;
- clear support contact;
- official-source links;
- security/privacy explanation.

No invented social proof.

Possible content:

## 16. Admin Space V3

Admin Space V2 remains the base.

V3 should improve service quality, not add decorative management features.

### A1 — Student case summary

Admin sees:
- current dossier stage;
- next student action;
- next AlmaGo action;
- deadline;
- blocking item;
- recent activity.

### A2 — Clear student-visible boundary

Every editable message/field should indicate:
- `Visible par l'étudiant`
or
- `Interne à AlmaGo`

### A3 — Source review support

For programme/university information:
- official URL;
- last reviewed date;
- reviewer;
- fields requiring re-check.

### A4 — Deadline review

Admin should identify:
- deadlines approaching;
- outdated source verification;
- incomplete official links.

### A5 — Service quality

Possible operational indicators based only on real data:
- documents waiting for review;
- applications needing action;
- unanswered student messages;
- outdated programme information.

Avoid productivity scoring of staff.

## 21. Professional content standards

Every public page must pass these checks:

### Truth
- Is every claim factually supported?
- Is a source available for official requirements?
- Is the wording free from guarantees?

### Clarity
- Can a student understand the page without specialist knowledge?
- Is the next action visible?
- Are German terms explained?

### Human tone
- Does it sound like a real service employee could have written it?
- Is technical jargon removed?

### Trust
- Can the user see who is responsible?
- Is the difference between AlmaGo and the official institution clear?
- Are important dates/source links visible?

## 25. Governance

V3 must not destabilise the existing 41/45 Master Plan.

Rules:
- existing A38/A43/A44/A45 gates remain authoritative;
- no new functionality is allowed to bypass those gates;
- implement V3 in bounded phases;
- one phase, branch and PR at a time;
- no secrets in repository/chat;
- no fake data, fake testimonials or fake partnerships;
- no admission/legal claims without evidence;
- official-source features require actual sources;
- source verification dates require real verification;
- Auth/RLS/Storage changes remain separately reviewed;
- copy quality is treated as a first-class acceptance criterion.

> Je comprends ce qu'AlmaGo fait pour moi.

> Je sais où en est mon dossier.

> Je sais ce que je dois faire maintenant.

> Je sais d'où viennent les informations importantes.

> a serious international student service — not a technology demo.
